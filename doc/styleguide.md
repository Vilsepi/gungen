
The weapon diagrams should be flat 2-dimensional, but detailed line drawings of the weapon, similarly to mechanical schematics or machining drawings. However, is should be slightly stylized towards modern and serious aesthetic, but slightly less engineery and boring as black-and-white engineering diagrams.

A reference of the style we are going for: [A reference picture](00-reference.jpg)

Color theme should use high-contrast, low-saturation dark theme. The background is dark, and most of the UI uses bright near-white lines. Colors on the weapon model should be mostly muted, but some UI icons or a single highlighted part may use stronger saturation to pop up.
